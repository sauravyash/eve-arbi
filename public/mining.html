Wormhole shortcuts

ConnectionFound bySeenExpiresUse

ESI doesn't keep a travel history, so this page can't look back at jumps you made before. What it can do: while you're signed in with Record my wormhole jumps on (character menu), every system change is recorded from then on. Two readings in a row in systems that no stargate joins mean you took a wormhole (or a jump bridge or cyno), and that pair becomes a shortcut here. Jumps through wormhole space count one per hole, and never count as high-sec. Order ranges still follow stargates, as in game.
