Wormhole shortcuts

ConnectionFound bySeenExpiresUse

ESI doesn't keep a travel history, so this page can't look back at jumps you made before. What it can do: while you're signed in with Record my wormhole jumps on (character menu), every system change is recorded from then on. Two readings in a row in systems that no stargate joins mean you took a wormhole (or a jump bridge or cyno), and that pair becomes a shortcut here, for every character you sign in with on this browser. Group mappers know far more: link a Wanderer map with its API token and all its connections count too. Shortcuts apply to jump totals on every page (untick Use on every page to turn them off). Jumps through wormhole space count one per hole, and never count as high-sec. Order ranges still follow stargates, as in game.
