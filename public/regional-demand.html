Demand is ESI's daily market history for each region (last 30 days; missing days count as no trades). Stock is the sell orders listed in the region now, from EVE Tycoon (player structures included if ticked), and can be a few minutes old. History is kept on the server and refreshed once a day after ESI's daily update, so looking an item up again costs nothing. Click a region for its 90-day chart and the stations selling it.

Hotspots are ESI's warzone systems (refreshed every half hour) and its kills for the last hour. Demand is per region, so a warzone region's volume includes trade away from the fighting; stock nearby only counts orders within the market radius. The staples scan fetches history for the warzone regions above only. Click a hotspot to open its region below the ranking, or a staple to look it up. The supply run supplies the hotspots you tick (the 3 hottest when none are), buying where the staples' orders are cheapest within reach and selling at each hotspot's busiest market. Its profit assumes everything sells at those prices. The hauls are from your last universe scan.
